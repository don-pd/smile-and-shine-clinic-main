Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins welcome, but appointments are recommended to avoid waiting. Call or WhatsApp us for a slot.

Are you open on Sundays?

No. We're closed on Sundays. For severe pain or swelling outside hours, visit the nearest hospital's dental emergency.

How much does treatment cost?

Costs vary by procedure. After your consultation, you'll get a written plan with clear pricing — no hidden charges.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Pediatric dentistry is one of our core services. We make children's visits calm, friendly and even fun.

Is root canal painful?

With modern anaesthesia and our gentle technique, most patients feel little to no discomfort. The pain people fear is usually from the infection — the procedure relieves it.

Do you offer EMI or instalments?

For extensive plans (implants, orthodontics), we can structure staged payments tied to treatment phases. Ask at the front desk.
